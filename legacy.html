Our Legacy

Journey to Greatness

Axetron Gaming isn’t just a name—it’s a movement that sparked a revolution in Bangladesh’s PUBG Mobile esports scene. Born on December 10, 2021, through the vision of Ishon Chakma, Axetron emerged when competitive PUBG Mobile in Bangladesh was still finding its ground. As one of the best professional esports organizations in the region, Axetron Gaming didn’t just join the wave—we became the wave. From early scrims in small lobbies to dominating national tournaments, we’ve grown into a name synonymous with professionalism, power, and performance. Under the leadership of Owner Niloy Chakma, backed by the tactical brilliance of Srestha Chowdhury and Diganta Saha, Axetron has transformed into a benchmark organization, both on and off the battleground. Our rise has been methodical—driven by passion, powered by discipline. Behind every operation and execution is the Vesio Aries Management Panel, led by esports architects Siam Hasan aka KiKi and Shamil Ahmed aka Deadeye—two masterminds steering the future of Axetron with unmatched precision and vision. We don’t just develop rosters—we develop legends. We don’t follow trends—we set them. Our brand is rooted in the culture of grind, glory, and growth, serving not just as a team, but as a catalyst for the entire Bangladeshi gaming community. From humble beginnings to a national powerhouse—this is Axetron Gaming. This is our legacy
